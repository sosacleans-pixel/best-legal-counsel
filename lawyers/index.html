How we work

  • Advertising and marketing only.Best Legal Net provides advertising and marketing services. The terms are set out in a written agreement before any work begins.
  • Flat advertising feesFees for our advertising services are set in advance in a written agreement. They do not depend on whether anyone contacts, hires, or pays your firm, or on the outcome of any matter, and Best Legal Net does not share in legal fees.
  • People choose.People who use Best Legal Net choose which participating attorney contacts them. Best Legal Net does not pick, rank, or recommend lawyers, and there is no bidding for placement.
  • Utah only.This service is currently open only to Utah lawyers and law firms.
  • Separate information.Information you send through this page is used only to respond to your firm and is not shared publicly or with marketing agents. Marketing agents never receive anyone's form information. A person's details go only to the participating attorney that person chooses.
  • Your own compliance.Each lawyer and law firm is responsible for its own compliance with Utah attorney advertising rules, the Utah Rules of Professional Conduct, and any Utah State Bar requirements. Best Legal Net does not give legal or ethics advice to lawyers. We suggest getting your own ethics guidance before you advertise with us.
  • No promises about results.We do not promise any number of inquiries, clients, or outcomes.
