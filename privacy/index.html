Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy is a draft for counsel review. It is not legal advice and does not create an attorney-client relationship.

Best Legal Net is an advertising and information service for people hurt in Utah car accidents. It is not a law firm and does not give legal advice. This policy explains how we handle information you submit through bestlegalnet.com. This service currently serves Utah only.

What this service is

Best Legal Net is not a law firm, is not a lawyer, and is not your lawyer. Sending a form does not create an attorney-client relationship with Best Legal Net, with any marketing agent, or with any law firm. Submissions are not confidential attorney-client communications.

What we collect

When you send a form, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, city or other location in Utah, and a description of what happened. The longer accident information form may also collect the injured person's date of birth, accident and injury details, treatment information, insurance information (not a Social Security number and not an insurance member ID), and optional information about a family member to reach if you asked to be contacted and you cannot be reached.

Health and accident details are collected only to forward a request for advertising and information. Please do not send Social Security numbers or other details we do not ask for.

Do not submit a child's information except as a parent or guardian. We do not ask for dates of birth of children or siblings.

How we use and share information

We use the information you submit to review your request and, if you ask us to, to share a facts-only summary or the form with participating independent Utah lawyers you may choose. Your name and contact information go only to a firm you agree to contact, or that you ask Best Legal Net to introduce. You may choose any lawyer, including lawyers who are not listed here.

We do not sell your information.

If you check the optional box asking us to contact you, we may contact you by phone, email, or WhatsApp about your form. We will not contact you, or your relatives, unless you ask us to.

Form processing

Forms on this website are processed by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which receives the information you enter so it can be delivered to Best Legal Net. FormSubmit is a vendor that processes the form. Submissions are currently delivered to Best Legal Net's office inbox at phoenixtax2022@gmail.com.

If you used a marketing agent's link, that marketing agent is told only that a submission happened and does not receive your information.

Access and deletion

To ask for access to the information we have from a form you sent, or to ask us to delete it, email phoenixtax2022@gmail.com and describe the request. We will review the request and respond as we reasonably can. We may need to keep some records if the law requires it or if a participating lawyer already received information you asked us to share.

Utah only

This service is currently open only for Utah. Please do not use the forms for accidents or legal matters outside Utah.

Changes

We may update this policy. The updated policy will be posted on this page.
