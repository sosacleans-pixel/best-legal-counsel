Why talk with a Utah attorney early

Every accident is different. A conversation with a lawyer can help you understand where you stand.

Questions about insurance

An attorney can explain how insurance may apply to your situation and what to expect when adjusters call.

Timing matters

Legal deadlines depend on the facts of each case. An attorney can tell you which ones may apply to you.

Keep your records

Photos, reports, and notes about your injuries can be useful. Ask the attorney what to bring.
